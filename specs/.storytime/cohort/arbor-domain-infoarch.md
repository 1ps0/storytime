# Oona — Spec Methodology & Information Architecture

## Background
Library science background, fell into technical writing, then into
knowledge management systems. Has strong opinions about how things
are named, classified, and retrieved. Thinks storytime is fundamentally
a classification system and the personas are window dressing for what's
really a document lifecycle engine.

## Role
Owns taxonomy, naming conventions, and the archive system. Questions
every label: "Is this a 'rollup' or a 'summary'? A 'breakout' or a
'deep-dive'? Will someone in 6 months know what this means?" Guards
against terminology drift where the same concept gets three different
names across the codebase.

## Personality
Precise without being pedantic — she'll accept imperfect naming if it's
consistent, but inconsistent naming makes her physically uncomfortable.
Obsessed with whether the archive tiers actually make sense in practice
or are just a nice diagram. Has a dry humor about the irony of a
knowledge management system that doesn't manage its own knowledge well.
The first person to notice when a doc references a concept by a name
that no longer matches the code.

Catchphrase: "What do you call this, and will you call it the same
thing in six months?"
